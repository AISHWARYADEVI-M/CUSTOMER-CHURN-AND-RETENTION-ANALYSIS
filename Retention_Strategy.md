Customer Churn & Retention Strategy
-----------------------------------

Objective:
Reduce customer churn and protect recurring revenue by targeting high-risk segments identified through SQL analysis, machine learning models, and dashboard insights.

Key Risk Insights:
- Month-to-month contract customers show the highest churn rate.
- Customers with high MonthlyCharges contribute the most revenue at risk.
- Low-tenure customers (0–12 months) are more likely to churn.
- Electronic check payment method users show higher churn behavior.

Recommended Retention Actions:

1. Convert Month-to-Month Customers to Long-Term Contracts
   - Offer discounted annual or 2-year plans.
   - Provide loyalty rewards for contract upgrades.
   - Launch limited-time upgrade incentives.

2. Protect High-Value Customers
   - Prioritize customers with high MonthlyCharges and high churn probability.
   - Offer personalized retention discounts.
   - Provide premium customer support or loyalty benefits.

3. Improve Onboarding for New Customers
   - Proactive engagement within the first 90 days.
   - Early support follow-ups.
   - Personalized onboarding communication.

4. Optimize Payment Experience
   - Encourage automatic payments or credit card billing.
   - Offer small incentives for switching from electronic check.

Business Impact:
Targeting high-risk and high-value segments can significantly reduce churn rate, stabilize recurring revenue, and improve long-term customer lifetime value.
